# ⚽ Jogo

> AI-Powered Football Performance Analysis & Talent Discovery Platform

Jogo is an AI-powered platform designed to bridge the gap between talented grassroots football players and professional opportunities.

The platform analyzes football videos using Artificial Intelligence and Computer Vision to generate objective performance insights, helping players improve while enabling clubs, academies, and scouts to discover talents through verified performance data.

---

# 🚀 Problem

Thousands of talented football players struggle to reach professional opportunities because talent discovery still depends heavily on:

- Personal connections
- Traditional scouting
- Coach recommendations
- Manual observation

As a result, many talented players remain undiscovered.

---

# 💡 Solution

Jogo provides:

- 🎥 AI-powered football video analysis
- 📊 Objective performance reports
- 📈 Player development tracking
- 👤 Digital football profiles
- 🔍 Talent discovery platform for clubs & scouts

---
